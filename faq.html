Frequently asked questions

Short answers about digital flyer campaigns, the AI Conversion Engine, and billing. Still wondering about something? Contact us or book a free demo.

Lead generation

Digital Flyers

What is a digital flyer?

It's your offer, designed as a flyer and distributed digitally across 15+ online platforms — social media, search and popular websites — instead of being printed and dropped in mailboxes. Interested people open your flyer page, where they can call you or leave their details, and those become your leads.

How is this different from running my own online ads?

With usual online ads you pay for clicks or impressions. With Adviuz you pay only when someone views your flyer for at least 25 seconds — real attention on your offer. Design, distribution and lead capture are all handled for you.

How much does a flyer campaign cost?

Campaigns are quoted individually, based on your area, your audience and how many viewed flyers you want. Request a quote on the pricing page and we'll walk you through it on a short call.

Do I have to design the flyer myself?

No. Our team designs it from your offer and brand, and nothing goes live until you approve it.

Where do my leads go?

Every lead lands in your Adviuz hub with an instant alert on your phone. If you also use the AI Conversion Engine, it starts following up right away.

Can I pause a campaign?

Yes, any time. Remaining views stay in your account for your full 6-month validity and can be restarted or applied to a new flyer or offer.

Lead conversion

AI Conversion Engine

What does the AI Conversion Engine do?

It responds to every new lead for you: an instant text, an AI voice call within 60 seconds, then follow-up by call, text and email until the lead books. It books appointments into your calendar during the call, sends reminders, and saves a recording, transcript and summary on the lead's timeline.

Where can the leads come from?

Any source — Adviuz flyer campaigns, Facebook and Instagram lead forms, your website forms, and inbound or missed calls to your AI line.

What do the plans cost?

They're prepaid one-time packs with no monthly fee: Essential $600 CAD (follows up with about 400 new leads, 120-day validity), Pro $1,000 CAD (about 800 leads, 180 days, setup included) and Max $2,000 CAD (about 2,000 leads, 360 days, priority setup and a monthly review call). One-time setup is $300, included with Pro and Max. You recharge only when you need more — full details on the pricing page.

Can I control what the AI says and when it works?

Yes. You choose the hours it works, hand-tune its greeting and answers, and switch autopilot on or off for any lead. You can listen live, join or take over any call, and you keep your normal line alongside the AI line, switching in one tap.

Is the calling and texting compliant?

The system contacts leads only within allowed hours in the lead's own time zone, honours opt-outs instantly, and pauses automation the moment a lead replies. Consent language is built into every Adviuz lead form. See our Terms and Privacy Policy for details.

What happens if I don't use all my credits?

Credits last until the end of your plan's validity period, and we send reminders before expiry. Expired credits are not refundable, so pick the pack that matches your lead flow — you can always recharge later.

Billing

Billing & refunds

Are there monthly fees?

No. AI Conversion Engine packs are one-time prepaid purchases, and flyer campaigns are quoted per campaign. You pay again only when you choose to recharge or run a new campaign.

Can I get a refund?

Before we start work — yes, in full. For flyer campaigns, after design starts but before launch, we refund minus a $300 design and setup fee. Once a campaign launches, or once your AI setup begins, payments are non-refundable because your budget is committed to the advertising platforms and provisioning costs. Delivery issues are always made right with continued delivery or credits. Full details in the Refund Policy.

When is a refund always given?

If we fail to launch your campaign or make your AI service live and you cancel; if the advertising platforms reject your flyer content despite reasonable efforts on both sides (the unlaunched portion); and for duplicate or clearly accidental payments — promptly, no questions asked.

How does auto-recharge work?

Automatic top-ups follow the same rules as any purchase, never happen more than once per day per campaign, and send an instant receipt and invoice. You can switch auto-recharge off in your hub at any time — that stops future charges.

How do I request a refund?

Email support@adviuz.com from the email address on your account, or call +1 647 250 1152, within 30 days. Approved refunds return to your original payment method through Stripe, typically within 5–10 business days. If a charge looks wrong, please talk to us first — almost every billing question is fixed in one conversation.

How do I pay?

Securely by card through Stripe. Adviuz never sees or stores your card details.
