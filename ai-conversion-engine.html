FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an Adviuz flyer campaign to use it?

No. The AI Conversion Engine works on its own, with leads from your own sources, such as Facebook and Instagram lead ads or leads you add yourself. If you also run Adviuz digital flyers, those leads flow in automatically.

What happens after I pay?

You get a payment receipt by email right away. Our team then contacts you to set up your account: your hub login, AI voice, phone number, follow-up plan and lead sources. Once it is set up, you log in at hub.adviuz.ca and the engine starts working on your leads.

How does billing work?

Plans are billed monthly by card through Stripe, in Canadian dollars. Your minutes and messages refresh every month. You can cancel any time, and your plan stays active until the end of the month you have paid for.

What if I use up my minutes early?

You can add a one-time top-up or move to a bigger plan at any time. Unused minutes and messages do not carry over to the next month.

Can I buy without a monthly plan?

Yes. One-time prepaid packs are available on the Pricing page, and larger volumes are available on request.

What is the difference between AI minutes and call minutes?

AI minutes are used when the AI talks to your leads on the phone. Call minutes cover regular calls you and your team make from the hub.

Will people know they are talking to an AI?

Yes. The AI identifies your business, and if someone asks for a person, your team is notified and can join or take over the call.

Can I use it for leads in the United States?

Yes. US calling and texting rules (TCPA) require that leads have agreed to be contacted. Tell us during setup and we will set up your consent wording and contact rules with you.
