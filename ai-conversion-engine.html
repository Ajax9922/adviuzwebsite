Built-in courtesy rules

Persistent, never annoying

Follow-up only works if it's welcome. The engine has hard rules it cannot break — for every lead, on every campaign.

These rules also keep your campaigns on the right side of Canadian anti-spam law (CASL): opt-outs are honored absolutely, and leads whose consent has expired are not contacted.

FAQ

Common questions

Will people know they're talking to an AI?

Yes. The engine is polite and clear, and it identifies your business. If someone asks for a human, your team is notified and can join or take over the call.

Isn't this just spamming people?

No — and the rules make sure of it. Only people who responded to your campaign are contacted, never more than two touches a day, only between 9 AM and 7 PM their time, every message has an opt-out, and opt-outs stop everything instantly.

Who writes the messages?

The texts and emails in your plan are written and approved when your campaign is set up, in your brand's voice — so you always know what leads receive. The AI writes one thing on its own: the single gentle nudge when a conversation goes quiet.

What happens when a lead replies?

All automated follow-up for that lead pauses immediately, and the conversation takes over — the AI or your team responds. The plan never talks over a live conversation.

Can my team take over a lead?

Any time. Flip that lead's Autopilot switch off and every automated plan stops on the spot. Everything the engine did is on the lead's timeline, so whoever takes over sees the full history.

Do I need a flyer campaign to use it?

The Conversion Engine is sold as an add-on to Adviuz flyer packages. It works best when it's connected to the campaigns bringing your leads in.
