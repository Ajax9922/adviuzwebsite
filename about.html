About Adviuz

Built so no lead gets ignored.

Adviuz is a Canadian advertising platform built by Instad Web Services Ltd. in Saskatoon, with roots going back to 2014. We put your flyer in front of the right people digitally — and make sure everyone who responds actually hears back.

Our story

We ran the campaigns first. Then we built the platform.

Adviuz didn't start as software. It started in 2014 as hands-on work — designing, launching and managing advertising campaigns for hundreds of businesses, one at a time: schools and colleges, real estate developers, clinics, gyms, retailers, restaurants, service providers.

Running that many campaigns teaches you two things. Businesses waste money on advertising nobody can measure. And the leads they do get go cold because nobody follows up fast enough. Adviuz is the answer to both — flyers you only pay for when they're viewed, and an AI engine that replies to every lead in seconds.
