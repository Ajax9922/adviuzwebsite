Our journey

From paper pamphlets to an AI
that answers every lead.

Adviuz began in 2014 with one idea — distribute pamphlets without printing them. Four product generations later, it's a Canadian platform run from Saskatoon, Saskatchewan, serving businesses across Canada and the US.

2014

Pamphlets, without the paper

The problem: printing costs, no edits once printed, no way to know if flyers were even delivered — let alone read — and mountains of paper waste.

What we built: digital pamphlet distribution. No printing cost, unlimited design edits, a 100% distribution guarantee, live tracking — and the rule we still run on today: you're only charged when someone views your flyer for 25 seconds.

Phase 2

Every lead in one place

The problem: campaigns worked — and advertisers drowned. Enquiries came in from every platform, and leads slipped through the cracks.

What we built: a lead management system that pulls every enquiry into one screen, with one-click calling, texting and WhatsApp — so nobody has to hunt for their own leads.

Phase 3

Simple enough for day one

The problem: staff turnover. Every new hire meant retraining on complicated software while leads waited.

What we built: an interface anyone with basic computer skills can run on their first day — so following up thousands of leads stopped depending on one trained person.

Today

Follow-up, solved

The problem: even with every lead in one place, the follow-up itself was the bottleneck. Leads go cold in minutes, and no team can answer at midnight.

What we built: the AI Conversion Engine — every lead gets a text and a call within seconds, follow-up runs for up to 6 months, and your team steps in to close. Built and run from Saskatoon for businesses across Canada and the US.
