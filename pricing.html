Questions

Pricing questions, answered.

What counts as a "viewed flyer"?

A flyer counts as viewed only when a person keeps it open for 25 seconds. Shorter opens don't count against your package.

Do packages expire?

Packages are valid for 6 months from purchase. Most campaigns finish well before that.

Is this a subscription?

Digital Flyer packages are one-time purchases — you buy a set number of viewed flyers. The AI Conversion Engine add-on is monthly, and you can review it at your demo.

Can I start smaller than $5,000?

Starter is our smallest package. If you're not sure it's right for your business, book a demo — we'll look at your goals together and tell you honestly whether digital flyers fit.

How do I pay?

By card, through Stripe. You'll receive a secure checkout link after your demo. All prices are in Canadian dollars.

What if my campaign doesn't deliver all my views?

Undelivered views are covered by our Refund Policy — you can get the undelivered portion refunded.
