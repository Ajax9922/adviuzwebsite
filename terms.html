1. What we provide

Adviuz distributes digital flyers to targeted audiences on behalf of clients, delivers the contact details of interested people ("leads") to the client's dashboard, and optionally provides the AI Conversion Engine — an add-on service that contacts a client's leads by text, email, and AI-assisted phone calls.

2. Viewed flyers

Packages are sold as a number of viewed flyers. A flyer counts as viewed only when a person keeps it open for 25 seconds. Impressions or opens shorter than 25 seconds are not charged against your package.

3. Packages and validity

Digital Flyer packages are one-time purchases and are valid for 6 months from the date of purchase. Views not delivered within that period are handled under our Refund Policy. The AI Conversion Engine is sold as prepaid packages of AI call minutes, texts, emails and regular call minutes. Each package is valid for the period stated at purchase (60 to 360 days); unused credits expire at the end of that period.
