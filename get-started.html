Is this a subscription?

No. It's a one-time prepaid payment with no monthly fees. Your plan is valid for 12 months and you can pause anytime.

Consumers or businesses: which should I pick?

Pick "consumers" if you sell to the public (for example clinics, real estate, retail, fitness). Pick "businesses" if you sell to other companies. Business audiences are smaller and harder to reach, so those plans include fewer views at the same price.

What do I need to get started?

Your logo, a few photos, your offer, phone number and website. We design the flyer and send it to you for approval.

How soon will my flyer go live?

Usually within a few business days of receiving your content and your design approval.
