Questions

Before you buy

Is this a subscription?

No. It's a one-time prepaid payment with no monthly fees. Complete Solution and Digital Flyers plans are valid for 12 months and you can pause anytime. AI Conversion Engine plans last 120 to 360 days, and you recharge only when you need more.

Which solution should I pick?

The Complete Solution brings you new enquiries and follows every one of them up. Pick the AI Conversion Engine alone if you already get leads and want each one followed up. Pick Digital Flyers alone if you have a team to follow up leads yourself. Not sure? Start with the Pilot.

What do I need to get started?

Your logo, a few photos, your offer, phone number and website. We design the flyer and send it to you for approval.

How soon will my flyer go live?

Usually within a few business days of receiving your content and your design approval.

How do I pay?

Choose your plan, enter your province, then pay by card or Apple Pay on a secure payment page. E-transfer is also available.
