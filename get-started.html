Built-in rules

Persistent, never pushy

The engine follows fixed contact rules for every lead, so follow-up stays welcome and within Canadian anti-spam law (CASL).

Everything runs from one hub: each lead's full timeline, a live call monitor, group texts, emails and calls to your own leads (Pro and up) and campaign reports. Your data is stored in Canada.

    Before you buy

    Is this a subscription?

    No. It's a one-time prepaid payment with no monthly fees. Plans are valid for 12 months and you can pause anytime. The Pilot is valid for 6 months.

    B2C or B2B: which do I pick?

    Pick B2C if your customers are people and families. Pick B2B if you sell to other businesses. The price is the same; B2B flyers reach business decision-makers, so the view and AI minute counts are adjusted.

    What happens after I pay?

    You get a receipt by email right away. Our team contacts you to collect your logo, photos and offer, designs your flyer and sets up your AI. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

    How do I pay?

    Pick your plan, enter your details and pay by card or Apple Pay on a secure Stripe page. Prefer e-transfer? Call us.
