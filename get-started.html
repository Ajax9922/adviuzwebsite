    Before you buy

    Is this a subscription?

    No. It's a one-time prepaid payment with no monthly fees. Complete Solution and flyer packages are valid for 12 months and you can pause anytime (the Pilot is valid for 6 months). AI Engine plans last 120 to 360 days, and you recharge only when you need more.

    Which one should I pick?

    The Complete Solution brings you new enquiries and follows every one up. Flyer Pricing is for businesses with their own team to follow up leads. AI Engine Pricing is for businesses that already get leads. Not sure? Start with the Pilot.

    B2C or B2B?

    Pick B2C if your customers are people and families. Pick B2B if you sell to other businesses: your flyer reaches business decision-makers, so each package includes fewer, more targeted views.

    What happens after I pay?

    You get a receipt by email right away. Our team contacts you for your logo, photos and offer, designs your flyer and sets up your AI. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

    Can I talk to someone first?

    Yes. Call +1 647 250 1152 (Mon–Fri, 10 AM–6 PM CST) or book a free demo. Prefer e-transfer? Call us.
